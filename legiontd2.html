How to read this chart & sources

B = favorable buy candidate for the unit’s role. U = favorable upgrade candidate. C = conditional, neutral, mixed, or dependent on your board. W = weak fit for its main role against the bare wave; it does not mean sell. – = excluded from the 250-gold wave-1 baseline or unavailable as a fresh form there.

Tank ratings emphasize incoming damage; damage-unit ratings emphasize outgoing damage. Ability exceptions are included. Support, stacking and summoning can require board-specific judgment. Wave 21 has no basic type advantage. These are analytical recommendations, not combat-simulator results or an economy schedule. Incoming mercenaries, placement and accumulated stacks still matter.

Sorting a wave puts B/U together first, then C, W, and excluded; click again to reverse. Unit, role, gold and total-value headings also sort. A focus wave automatically sorts by its rating. Your filters are remembered locally when your browser permits it.
